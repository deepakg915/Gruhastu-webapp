App Name:Grihastu
Meaning:
Derived from Grihastha (गृहस्थ) — the stage of life centered around family, responsibility, values, and dharma.
The name represents a platform that brings Hindu wisdom into modern daily life.

Mission Statement

Grihastu aims to preserve, simplify, and spread the timeless wisdom of 
Hindu culture, scriptures, traditions, and spiritual practices through modern technology and AI.

The platform is designed to help people  — understand why traditions exist,not just what to do.
Main Features
1. Epic Stories Section
2. AI Spiritual Chatbot
3. Stotras & Shlokas Library
5. Festivals & Traditions,History,Mythological background
6. Hindu Lifestyle Guidance,Ayrveda,Yoga,Meditation
